Discussion image

Cliff lighting and boardwalk arrival

A night-time arrival idea with warm cliff illumination, a foreshore boardwalk, planting, low seating and a sheltered rest point.

Good review questions: cultural permissions, light spill, wildlife impact, maintenance, public safety, storm exposure and whether the arrival path still works during ferry peaks.

Plan-style prompt

Foreshore route plan

A map-like concept that strings together arrival paths, a coastal garden, lookout, boardwalk nodes and a plaza-style welcome point.

Good review questions: land tenure, tide levels, access widths, wheelchair gradients, seawall constraints, habitat impact and how the route connects back to the ferry terminal works.

Day view

Daytime foreshore promenade

A calmer daytime version of the edge: stone seating, planting, a patterned path, low barriers and a boardwalk along the water.

Good review questions: heat, shade, slip resistance, drainage, erosion, clear sightlines, seating placement and how much open ground the terminal still needs.

Evening view

Evening cultural-lighting promenade

A more atmospheric version of the foreshore walk, using lighting, stone edges and cliff textures to make the ferry-gateway feel cared for after dark.

Good review questions: dark-sky limits, power, lighting control, cultural review, flood/storm resilience, night safety and what should stay quiet for wildlife.

Send an example

Community images can be added through the public channels.

This is a curated static site, so visitors cannot upload straight onto the page. To suggest an image for this gallery, post it on the DGFTU Facebook thread or send it through the contact form with the image, credit name and short idea note.

Include the source

Name the photo, map point or view you started from. If the image is invented without a source view, say that clearly.

Choose a channel

Post it on the Facebook thread for public discussion, or use the contact form if you want to send it directly for review before it appears here.

Credit the maker

Say exactly how the contributor should be credited, and whether a profile link, group mention or plain name is OK to display.

Label the idea

Say what the image is testing and what review it needs: access, ecology, cultural review, ferry operations, cost, maintenance or another check.

Useful label: "Community GenAI concept image for discussion. Not an approved plan."

Evidence map

A rough site walk from community 360 photos.

The points below come from rounded GPS in GoPro Max 360 photos. They are good enough to prompt discussion and early world-building, but they are not cadastral, engineering or survey data.

Photo points

Pick a point, then inspect the flattened panorama.

The map plane is schematic. It intentionally avoids pretending the current prototype is more precise than it is.

Panorama strip

The selected 360 image becomes a prompt seed.

World builders usually prefer clean spatial inputs. These 2:1 equirectangular images are a useful start for tools that accept panoramas, and a useful visual reference for tools that do not.

What to do with this

Turn the map into useful questions.

Access and movement

Where do pedestrians, ferry passengers, buses, barge traffic, kiss-and-ride cars and bikes currently collide or slow each other down?

Shade and waiting

Which waiting areas feel exposed, uncomfortable or hard to understand, and what canopies or landscape ideas would actually help?

Culture and arrival

What makes the arrival feel specifically Gumpi and Minjerribah rather than generic ferry infrastructure?

Open data ask

Which official source files would improve the community simulation: CAD, GIS layers, bathymetry, ground survey, LiDAR, traffic counts or constraints maps?
